Overview

A series of seven residential interiors: living room, master bedroom, girl's bedroom, boy's bedroom, guest bathroom, master bathroom and kitchenette. Each space is presented with an overall view and close-up detail renders.

Design approach

Developed each interior around a restrained palette of timber paneling, stone, and soft textiles, coordinating furniture placement, wall treatments and finishes within one consistent design language.

Modeled in 3ds Max and rendered with V-Ray. Lighting combines daylight through large windows with warm integrated LED strips and lit shelving, and the detail renders focus on materials, textiles and furniture.

Community Recycling Center

Project type
Architectural Design
Context
University design studio project
Location
Al-Bireh industrial zone
Images
56

Overview

A community recycling center proposed for an existing workshop building in the Al-Bireh industrial zone. It responds to solid waste problems in Palestine by combining recycling, education and creative reuse: workshops for children and youth, exhibitions of products made from recycled materials, a bazaar, a café and administration.

Design approach

The proposal starts from analysis of the site, the neighborhood workshops and the waste each one produces, and the areas of the existing building. A zoning and circulation strategy across ground and first floors organizes production, exhibition, social, service and administration zones.

Presentation

Presented on five A0 boards: problem statement and site analysis, user experience and zoning, site plan and machines, plans, and detail drawings for garden furniture, a steel bridge and the roof.
